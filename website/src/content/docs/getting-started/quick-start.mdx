This walkthrough takes you from an empty screen to a finished meeting report. It assumes OpenSpeak is already running — if not, see [Install & self-host](/getting-started/self-hosting/) first.

<Steps>

1. **Create an agenda.**

   Open **Agendas** in the header and click **New agenda**. Give it a title — a description, start date/time, and timezone are optional (the timezone defaults to your browser's). Saving drops you straight into the agenda editor.

   {/* TODO screenshot: new agenda form */}

2. **Add a few items.**

   Use the row at the bottom of the editor: type a title, set the **Expected** time in minutes (Min and Max are optional), and click **Add**. The form refocuses automatically, so you can type out a whole agenda without touching the mouse.

   {/* TODO screenshot: agenda editor with items */}

3. **Assign people (optional).**

   Open **participants** to add names to the roster, then pick an assignee on each item — or click an item's "Unassigned" link for a one-tap assign dialog.

4. **Open the two meeting screens.**

   Click **Run meeting** to open the **display screen** — put this one on the projector; it shows the planned schedule until the meeting starts. From its header, open the **Control page** on your phone — this is the remote that actually drives the meeting.

5. **Run the meeting.**

   Tap **Start meeting** on the phone. The first item's timer starts immediately, everywhere. As the meeting proceeds:

   - Tap **Finish agenda item** when an item is done, then **Start agenda item** when the next one begins. The gap in between is normal — applause, walking to the lectern — and is tracked separately.
   - The timer turns **green** at the minimum time, **yellow** at the expected time, and **red** shortly before the maximum, when the phone also beeps.
   - **Pause**, **Skip**, and **End meeting** are right there if you need them.

   {/* TODO screenshot: control screen during a running item */}

6. **Read the report.**

   Tap **End meeting** (twice — destructive buttons need a confirming second tap) and both screens land on the meeting report: actual vs. planned time for every item, with statuses like *on time*, *over expected*, or *skipped*.

   {/* TODO screenshot: run report */}

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
Any screen can be reloaded, closed, or opened on another device mid-meeting without losing anything — the meeting state lives entirely on the server.
</Aside>
