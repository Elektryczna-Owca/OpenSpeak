## Why OpenSpeak

<CardGrid stagger>
	<Card title="Agendas in minutes" icon="pencil">
		Drag-and-drop editor with min / expected / max time for every item.
		Paste an agenda straight from a spreadsheet — CSV import just works.
	</Card>
    <Card title="Open source, community project" icon="heart">
        This is free and Open Source project. Help it grow: use it, report ideas on [github](https://github.com/Elektryczna-Owca/OpenSpeak/issues)
        or contact me directly - I have Google's email that has and address part "nexor1984".
    </Card>
	<Card title="Built for Toastmasters" icon="star">
		Speaker rounds, role assignments, and a ready-made club meeting
		template with prepared speeches, Table Topics, and evaluations.
		Support for ad-hoc speaker you can assign a participant on the fly.
	</Card>
	<Card title="A timer everyone can see" icon="approve-check-circle">
		Projector display with the classic green–yellow–red progression,
		"up next" prompts, and a QR code for the current item's link.
		Useful for linking to evaluation forms.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Run it from your phone" icon="rocket">
		Start, pause, skip, and finish items from any phone. A discreet beep
		tells the controller when a speaker hits maximum time.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Reporting" icon="document">
		Actual vs. planned for every item and speaker — including the idle
		time between items. History never changes after the fact.
	</Card>
	<Card title="No accounts, no lock-in, no tracking" icon="open-book">
		Share a link and go — no logins to manage, no cookies to track you. Open source, self-hosted
		with Docker and PostgreSQL on your own server.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Projector-display friendly" icon="desktop">
		Different color themes to let you choose the best contrast for your projector.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
